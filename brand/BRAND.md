# Cellar brand

**Cellar = cell + cellar.** Your phone is the storeroom downstairs: everything you keep is down there, and Cellar is the door to it, opened from your Mac.

## The mark: the cellar door

A low arched door with four steps cut out of it, going down.

- The door is one solid colour. The steps are holes, not drawn lines, so whatever is behind the door shows through them.
- **The door takes the colour of the current theme.** In the app it uses the theme accent; on Graphite it uses the text colour (white on dark, black on light).
- No container. The door stands on its own. The only exception is the macOS app icon, because macOS puts every Dock icon in a tile.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `assets/cellar-mark.svg` | The door in `currentColor`, for the web |
| `assets/cellar-mark-black.svg`, `-white.svg` | Fixed colours, for docs and print |
| `assets/cellar-wordmark.svg` (+ `-black`, `-white`) | Door + "Cellar", outlined, no font needed |
| `assets/app-icon.svg`, `app-icon-1024.png` | macOS icon: white door on a graphite tile |
| `assets/menubar-*.svg` | Menu bar: filled door = phone connected, outline = no phone |
| `assets/favicon.svg` | Black in a light browser, white in a dark one |
| `assets/dmg-background.*` | The DMG window (light, so Finder's black labels stay readable). Made by hand from the wordmark; rebuild the `.tiff` with `tiffutil -cathidpicheck` |

Every other file is generated by `make_brand.py` (needs `fonttools` and `rsvg-convert`). Change the door there, not in the SVGs. The script also writes `mac/AppIcon.iconset` and the `mac/Menubar*Template.png` files.

### Spacing and size

- Clear space around the mark: at least the height of the top step's gap (about 1/7 of the door's height).
- Smallest size: 12 px tall. Below 16 px, drop the wordmark and use the door alone.
- In the wordmark, the door is 1.06 × the cap height, sits on the baseline, and is followed by a gap of 0.3 × cap height.

### Don't

- Don't put the door in a box, circle or badge (except the app icon).
- Don't outline, rotate, stretch or add a shadow to the door.
- Don't fill the steps with a second colour; they're holes.
- Don't use more than one door colour in the same view.

## Colour

The door follows the app's themes. Neutrals carry everything else.

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Cellar black | `#111111` | Graphite background, dark text |

## Type

- **Funnel Display** SemiBold (600) for the wordmark and big headlines. Tracking −1 to −3 %. OFL licence; the file is in `brand/fonts/`.
- **Geist** for everything else: UI, body, buttons. Already in `fonts/`.
- Never set body text in Funnel Display.

## Voice

Plain, short, calm. Say what happens, in the user's words.

- "Copied 12 files to Camera", not "Transfer completed successfully!"
- "Plug in your phone", not "Please connect your Android device to continue."
- Name the phone's places the way Android does: Internal storage, Download, DCIM, Camera.
- Sentence case. No exclamation marks. No "seamless", "unlock", "empower".
- Lead with privacy only where it matters: "Everything stays on your Mac."

**Tagline:** Your Android phone, on your Mac.
**Short line:** Browse, copy and back up your phone. Nothing leaves your Mac.
