        Connect over Wi-Fi

        Your phone and Mac need to be on the same Wi-Fi network. You only pair once; after that Cellar reconnects by itself.

        Making a code…
        1. On the phone, open Settings › Developer options.
        2. Turn on Wireless debugging, then tap it to open it.
        3. Tap Pair device with QR code and scan this code.
        1. On the phone, open Settings › Developer options › Wireless debugging and turn it on.
        2. Tap Pair device with pairing code. Enter the 6-digit code and the IP address & port shown in that popup.

        Use this for a phone you've paired before, or one you switched to wireless with the cable. On Android 10 and older: plug in once and use Go wireless in the sidebar.
